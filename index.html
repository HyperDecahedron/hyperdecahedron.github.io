Natalia Sempere

Mixed Reality and Robotics Engineer

I like developing end-to-end HCI projects, from building the virtual environment to integrating the hardware.

Most of my work has involved some kind of Mixed Reality. I've worked with Meta Quest and HoloLens, but I also have experience with robot programming, machine learning, UX design and user testing.

I have research experience across multiple universities and laboratories, including KTH Royal Institute of Technology, the University of Alicante, and the University of Skövde.

MicroVRide: the 4-in-1 simulator

I worked on a VR micromobility simulator that supports four different vehicles. I developed two cybersickness mitigation techniques: an airflow accessory for the Meta Quest 2 and a virtual helmet.

TonguePlay

I developed a novel tongue-based input for VR using EMG sensors under the chin. The model predicts the tongue position (left, front, or right) and pressure (0 to 100). With it, users play two VR games using their tongue.

  • VR
  • EMG
  • Random Forest Regressor
  • OpenBCI

Olympic vs Paralympic Public Opinion: A Big Data Analysis

As part of a group project, we investigated the presence of social bullying directed at Olympic and Paralympic athletes on online platforms. I was in charge of collecting data from YouTube, and then used Google's Perspective API, VADER, TextBlob, and OpenAI's ChatGPT models to classify comments by sentiment and toxicity.

  • Big Data
  • Paralympics
